# 5 tiêu đề và 5 prompt thumbnail — tập 40 Flores

Đề xuất dùng tiêu đề 1 cho bản tiếng Anh chính. Chữ thumbnail thêm trong phần mềm thiết kế, không giao mô hình ảnh tự viết chữ. Mỗi ảnh 16:9, chừa khoảng trống cho 2–4 từ, kiểm tra ở kích thước điện thoại.

## 1. Flores: The Cave That Changed Our Human Story

Tiếng Việt: **Flores: Hang động thay đổi câu chuyện về loài người**.

Chữ gợi ý: `A DIFFERENT HUMAN STORY` / `MỘT QUÁ KHỨ KHÁC`.

Prompt: Create a compelling 16:9 documentary YouTube thumbnail, photorealistic illustration inspired by the broad limestone entrance of Liang Bua on Flores, eastern Indonesia. A pale limestone arch frames deep shadow; a single generic replica stone flake lies in sharp macro foreground on a neutral museum support, visually connected to softly visible sediment layers. Warm daylight against cool cave shadows, strong readable silhouette, one clear focal point, restrained cinematic contrast, scientifically plausible rock. Leave clean dark space on the upper right for a short title added later. No text, letters, logos or watermarks. No people, children, babies, teenagers, prehistoric human figures, miniature people, human remains, fantasy creatures or invented discoveries. This is an explanatory composite illustration, not an excavation photograph.

## 2. An Island Apart: Flores and Homo Floresiensis

Tiếng Việt: **Flores biệt lập: Hòn đảo và Homo floresiensis**.

Chữ gợi ý: `AN ISLAND APART` / `HÒN ĐẢO BIỆT LẬP`.

Prompt: Design a striking 16:9 documentary thumbnail illustrating Flores as an island separated by deep blue sea. Elevated view of a plausible elongated volcanic island with green ridges, a distant neighboring coastline across a wide water gap, subtle atmospheric haze, dramatic natural side lighting, the island as the dominant focal shape. Use an inset-free composition with a small unmarked limestone texture in the foreground to hint at the cave story. No land bridge, raft, ship, speculative migration arrows or false exact cartography. Reserve uncluttered ocean in the left third for editor-added title. No text, logos, watermarks, people, children, babies, teenagers, humanoid reconstructions, skeletons or fantasy imagery. Clearly an interpretive geographic illustration, not a satellite survey.

## 3. Inside Flores: Caves, Volcanoes, and an Unexpected Past

Tiếng Việt: **Bên trong Flores: Hang động, núi lửa và quá khứ bất ngờ**.

Chữ gợi ý: `BENEATH FLORES` / `BÊN DƯỚI FLORES`.

Prompt: Create a cinematic 16:9 travel-science thumbnail for Flores, eastern Indonesia. View outward from a plausible limestone cave mouth toward layered green volcanic hills, with a strong distant conical mountain shape inspired by Inerie. The cave frame occupies one side, the sunlit landscape the other; bold depth, natural scale, rich but believable greens, warm rim light, no active eruption. One coherent interpretive composite rather than a claim that this exact view exists from Liang Bua. Leave clear sky space for a brief title added later. No text, logos, watermarks, people, children, babies, teenagers, prehistoric figures, human remains or invented ruins.

## 4. Why Flores Made Human Evolution More Complicated

Tiếng Việt: **Vì sao Flores khiến câu chuyện tiến hóa của loài người phức tạp hơn?**

Chữ gợi ý: `THE STORY CHANGED` / `CÂU CHUYỆN ĐỔI KHÁC`.

Prompt: Produce a high-impact 16:9 science-documentary thumbnail: a sharply lit tabletop sediment model showing an older layered block beside a younger deposited wedge, one generic replica stone tool on a neutral support, dark limestone background, angled macro camera, precise tactile textures. Composition communicates that context changes interpretation, without numerical labels, a fake fossil, a claimed archaeological section or an invented discovery. Warm focused light on the model, cool restrained background, large clean negative space on the right for title added by an editor. No text, logos, watermarks, people, children, babies, teenagers, humanoid silhouettes, human bones, skulls or fantasy motifs. Clearly an illustrative museum model.

## 5. From Liang Bua to Kelimutu: Reading the Island of Flores

Tiếng Việt: **Từ Liang Bua đến Kelimutu: Đọc lịch sử đảo Flores**.

Chữ gợi ý: `READ THE ISLAND` / `ĐỌC LỊCH SỬ ĐẢO`.

Prompt: Create an inviting 16:9 documentary thumbnail focused on the neighboring crater-lake basins of Kelimutu on Flores, eastern Indonesia. Elevated oblique view, plausible volcanic crater walls, muted turquoise-green water and a darker neighboring basin under soft highland mist, natural morning light, realistic scale and visible terrain separating basins. Do not force all lakes into artificial perfect circles, promise permanent lake colors, depict instant color change, lava or an eruption. A narrow limestone-texture border subtly links the lake to the film's cave theme as an editorial composite. Leave calm misty sky for a short title added later. No text, logos, watermarks, people, children, babies, teenagers, prehistoric human figures or remains.
