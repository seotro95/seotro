# 5 tiêu đề và 5 prompt thumbnail — tập 85 Angkor

Đề xuất chọn tiêu đề 1 cho bản tiếng Anh chính. Mỗi prompt ảnh 16:9, bố cục rõ trên màn hình điện thoại. Chữ gợi ý được thêm trong phần mềm thiết kế; không yêu cầu mô hình ảnh tự viết chữ.

## 1. Angkor: The City Built Around Water

Tiếng Việt: **Angkor: Thành phố được định hình bởi nước**.

Chữ gợi ý: `BEYOND THE TEMPLES` / `PHÍA SAU NHỮNG NGÔI ĐỀN`.

Prompt: Create a compelling 16:9 documentary YouTube thumbnail about Angkor's city and water network. Recognizable Angkor Wat towers form a strong silhouette beyond a calm moat, while a subtle foreground transition reveals an unlabelled illustrative terrain model of channels and a rectangular reservoir. Warm stone against cool blue-green water, natural light, one dominant focal point and strong readable depth. This is an explanatory editorial composite, not an aerial survey or evidence that every channel connects to the temple moat. Leave clean space in the left third for a short title added later. No text, numbers, logos or watermarks. No people, children, babies, teenagers, miniature human figures, childlike statues, invented ruins or fantasy architecture.

## 2. The Water Network Behind Angkor's Towers

Tiếng Việt: **Mạng lưới nước phía sau những ngọn tháp Angkor**.

Chữ gợi ý: `FOLLOW THE WATER` / `LẦN THEO DÒNG NƯỚC`.

Prompt: Design a striking 16:9 science-history thumbnail with a clearly illustrative oblique terrain model inspired by the greater Angkor plain. A large rectangular reservoir, several physically plausible downhill channels and small neutral temple volumes form a legible connected composition. One blue water route catches the light from foreground to distance, with restrained earthy greens and sandstone colors. No exact archaeological map claim, labels, invented dates, underground x-ray or impossible uphill flow. Leave uncluttered dark space at upper right for editor-added title. No text, logos, watermarks, people, children, babies, teenagers, tiny human models or sacred ceremonies.

## 3. Too Much Water, Too Little Water: Understanding Angkor

Tiếng Việt: **Khi thừa nước, lúc thiếu nước: Hiểu về Angkor**.

Chữ gợi ý: `RAIN. DROUGHT. CHANGE.` / `MƯA, HẠN VÀ BIẾN ĐỔI`.

Prompt: Create an engaging 16:9 documentary thumbnail using a clearly editorial two-condition composition of a generic Angkor-inspired water landscape. One side shows heavy seasonal rain entering a safe earth channel, the other a modest dry channel with low water, with a single restrained temple silhouette linking the theme in the background. Natural textures, no apocalypse, no giant waves, no collapsing temple, no claim of a specific filmed ancient disaster or climate as the sole cause of historical change. Strong readable contrast without oversaturated colors. Reserve a quiet top band for a brief title added later. No text, logos, watermarks, people, children, babies, teenagers, miniature human figures or invented inscriptions.

## 4. Angkor Was More Than Its Temples

Tiếng Việt: **Angkor còn rộng lớn hơn những ngôi đền**.

Chữ gợi ý: `A CITY BEYOND STONE` / `THÀNH PHỐ NGOÀI ĐÁ`.

Prompt: Produce a cinematic 16:9 history-documentary thumbnail. A close sandstone gateway frames an illustrative low-density inhabited landscape model with simple house volumes, ponds, rice parcels, paths and a distant temple precinct. Clearly communicate the relationship between monumental stone and ordinary living spaces, without claiming an exact reconstructed neighborhood or population. Warm angled light, tactile stone, restrained depth of field, one focused foreground edge and readable broad arrangement beyond. Leave clear space on the right for later typography. No text, logos, watermarks, people, children, babies, teenagers, miniature humans, fantasy ruins or staged worship.

## 5. Why Angkor Changed: Water, Climate, and a Living City

Tiếng Việt: **Vì sao Angkor đổi thay: Nước, khí hậu và một thành phố đang sống**.

Chữ gợi ý: `MORE THAN ONE CAUSE` / `NHIỀU NGUYÊN NHÂN`.

Prompt: Create a thoughtful but visually arresting 16:9 documentary thumbnail. In sharp foreground, a clearly illustrative earth-bank model shows a repaired section beside a slightly eroded section; behind it, an unlabelled water channel leads toward recognizable Angkor-inspired temple architecture in soft natural light. Emphasize maintenance, adaptation and connection rather than instant collapse. One focused point of contrast on the repaired bank, muted greens and warm sandstone, realistic materials and plausible water scale. No claim of a specific excavated sequence, single final catastrophe or complete abandonment. Leave clean upper-left space for title added later. No text, logos, watermarks, people, children, babies, teenagers, tiny human figures or invented inscriptions.
