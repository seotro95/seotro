# 5 tiêu đề và 5 prompt thumbnail tương ứng

Tiêu đề chính bằng tiếng Anh; bản Việt giúp chọn ý. Ảnh thu nhỏ 16:9, không trẻ em. Chữ gợi ý nên chèn khi thiết kế, không yêu cầu mô hình vẽ chữ.

1. **Nan Madol: How a City Rose from a Lagoon**  
   Việt: **Nan Madol: Thành phố đã vươn lên từ đầm phá như thế nào?**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **BUILT ON WATER?**  
   Prompt: `Create a compelling 16:9 YouTube documentary thumbnail of Nan Madol, Pohnpei: a massive but realistically scaled wall of dark interlocking prismatic basalt beams rises from a shallow green-blue tidal channel; a low near-water viewpoint, wet stone sharply lit by warm dawn light, tropical vegetation and a second enclosure receding behind it. Make one striking wall corner the focal point; realistic weight, visible stable islet foundation, rich dark-versus-light separation, natural restrained colors, leave the upper-left quarter calm for text added later. No people, no children, no babies, no teenagers, no generated text, logos or watermarks. No floating rocks, submerged palace, aliens, treasure or fantasy architecture.`

2. **The Stone City That Turned Water into Power**  
   Việt: **Thành phố đá biến mặt nước thành quyền lực**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **WATER & POWER**  
   Prompt: `Create a cinematic yet geographically plausible 16:9 documentary thumbnail of Nan Madol's monumental basalt entrance across a narrow tidal channel, viewed at adult eye level from a safe exterior position. The channel leads the eye into the dark threshold, heavy crosswise basalt courses dominate the right side, soft tropical sunlight illuminates the water, palms frame the distance. Convey controlled access and architectural authority through composition, not invented guards or royalty. Strong single focal point, crisp near stone, calm negative space at upper left for later typography. No people, children, babies or teenagers; no text, logos, crowns, ritual reenactment, opened graves or fantasy elements.`

3. **99 Islands, One Question: Who Built Nan Madol?**  
   Việt: **99 đảo, một câu hỏi: Ai đã xây Nan Madol?**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **99 STONE ISLANDS**  
   Prompt: `Create a clear 16:9 YouTube history-and-geography thumbnail: an illustrative high oblique view of Nan Madol-style artificial stone islets separated by shallow tidal channels beside tropical Pohnpei. Emphasize several readable enclosures rather than attempting to display exactly ninety-nine islands; one large basalt wall corner anchors the lower foreground, a network of channels leads into the distance. Realistic dark basalt, coral-rubble ground, green palms, quiet luminous water. This is an interpretive composition, not an exact archaeological survey. Leave clean sky or water space for added lettering. No humans, no children of any age, no text, no map labels, no logos, no aliens, no implausible city skyline.`

4. **Inside Nan Madol: Basalt, Tides and an Ancient Center of Power**  
   Việt: **Bên trong Nan Madol: Đá bazan, thủy triều và trung tâm quyền lực cổ**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **HOW WAS THIS BUILT?**  
   Prompt: `Design a photorealistic 16:9 documentary thumbnail focused on a close basalt wall corner at Nan Madol, alternating massive natural prismatic beams crossing at right angles; low raking light reveals irregular contact surfaces and true heavy material texture. A quiet shallow tidal channel and soft tropical forest remain visible to one side, balancing intimate construction detail with place. One dominant corner silhouette, no collage, realistic scale, dramatic but believable lighting, leave uncluttered left-side room for post-production text. No people, children, babies or teenagers, no generated writing, logos, impossible stone fitting, metal machines, levitation or speculative construction reenactment.`

5. **Nan Madol's Greatest Mystery Begins with One Stone**  
   Việt: **Bí ẩn Nan Madol bắt đầu từ một khối đá**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **ONE STONE. A CITY.**  
   Prompt: `Create an emotionally engaging 16:9 documentary thumbnail at the Nan Madol waterline: a single wet dark basalt beam in the immediate foreground is sharply detailed, the camera looks along it toward an imposing interlocking wall and quiet channel fading into tropical dusk. A small natural ripple catches a warm reflection, connecting the tiny detail to the wider city. Calm mystery, strong foreground-to-background depth, realistic tidal lagoon rather than deep open ocean, muted emerald and charcoal palette, leave upper-left negative space for title lettering added later. Absolutely no people or children, no babies or teenagers, no text, symbols, logos, glowing artifacts, underwater treasure or supernatural effects.`

Ưu tiên tiêu đề 1 cho bản chính: nêu đúng câu hỏi mở và không hứa đã giải quyết mọi chi tiết vận chuyển. Cả năm hình đều là thumbnail đề xuất, không được ghi là ảnh khảo cổ thực địa.
