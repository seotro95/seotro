# 5 tiêu đề và 5 prompt thumbnail — tập 37

Tiêu đề Anh cho video chính, kèm bản Việt. Ảnh 16:9; chữ gợi ý chèn sau bằng công cụ thiết kế.

1. **Pohnpei: Where Rain Connects Forests, Rivers and Reefs**  
   Việt: **Pohnpei: Nơi mưa nối rừng, sông và rạn san hô**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **FOLLOW THE RAIN**  
   Prompt: `Create a compelling photorealistic 16:9 documentary thumbnail of Pohnpei, Federated States of Micronesia. A dark green volcanic ridge disappears into a soft low rain cloud; a forested valley leads naturally toward a sheltered turquoise lagoon and plausible reef edge. One coherent elevated coastal view, believable island scale, luminous water contrasted with shaded forest, restrained tropical colors, a single clear visual route from cloud to coast. Leave clean upper-left negative space for typography added later. No people, children, babies or teenagers; no text, logos, fantasy geography, erupting volcano, giant waterfall or disaster scene.`

2. **Beyond Nan Madol: The Living Island of Pohnpei**  
   Việt: **Phía sau Nan Madol: Hòn đảo Pohnpei còn sống**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **BEYOND THE STONES**  
   Prompt: `Create a striking 16:9 documentary thumbnail from outside Nan Madol: a heavy dark basalt wall corner occupies the near right foreground above a shallow tidal channel, while tropical vegetation and Pohnpei's green slopes open into the distance. Use depth to connect ancient masonry with a living island rather than isolating the ruin. Warm grazing light on stone, soft cloud over the hills, clear focal point, quiet left-side room for later lettering. Realistic prismatic basalt beams and stable artificial-islet foundation. No humans or children of any age, no generated words, logos, floating stones, treasure, underwater palace or invented ceremony.`

3. **Follow One Rainstorm across Pohnpei**  
   Việt: **Theo một cơn mưa đi qua Pohnpei**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **ONE RAINSTORM**  
   Prompt: `Design a photorealistic 16:9 nature-documentary thumbnail: an intimate sharply focused tropical leaf carries one large natural raindrop in the foreground, with layered humid forest and a small distant stream softly receding behind it. The reflected light in the droplet provides the focal point, rich natural leaf texture, calm mystery, credible scale, no impossible miniature world inside the drop. Keep the upper-left area simple for post-production text. No people, children, babies, teenagers, generated lettering, logos, fantasy animals, neon glow or catastrophic flooding.`

4. **Pohnpei's Hidden Map: From Mountain Clouds to Coral**  
   Việt: **Tấm bản đồ ẩn của Pohnpei: Từ mây núi đến san hô**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **CLOUD TO CORAL**  
   Prompt: `Create a clearly illustrative 16:9 documentary thumbnail of a single island section inspired by Pohnpei: cloud-wrapped green mountain, tree-covered slope, a modest stream, mangrove margin and shallow coral reef arranged in a physically readable continuous landscape model. High-quality natural-material rendering, subtle water route rather than arrows, strong composition, distinguish it visually as an explanatory model, not a precise surveyed map or impossible real photograph. Leave a calm corner for later text. No people, children, labels, generated writing, logos, underground city, pipes draining the whole island, fantasy caverns or climate prediction.`

5. **Rain, Forests and Ancient Places: Inside Pohnpei**  
   Việt: **Mưa, rừng và dấu tích cổ: Bên trong Pohnpei**  
   Chữ ngắn tùy chọn: **INSIDE POHNPEI**  
   Prompt: `Create an inviting 16:9 geography-documentary thumbnail framed through wet tropical leaves toward a sheltered channel and dark weathered basalt enclosure at Nan Madol. Green island slopes fade behind it into low cloud. Emphasize three readable layers: living forest, human-built stone and moving water. Restrained emerald and charcoal palette, soft warm light, realistic stable wall construction, one dominant focal point rather than a crowded collage; reserve clean left-side space for lettering added later. No people, no children of any age, no text, logos, invented inscriptions, royal actors, opened graves or supernatural elements.`

Ưu tiên tiêu đề 1 cho mạch từ mây đến rạn. Tiêu đề 2 nối được với tập Nan Madol trước mà vẫn nêu phạm vi mới của tập này.
