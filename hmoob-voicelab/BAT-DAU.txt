HMOOB VOICELAB 1.1 - TAO GIONG HMONG NAM / NU

BAN WINDOWS PORTABLE

1. Giai nen TOAN BO goi tai ve vao mot thu muc ban co quyen ghi.
   Khong chay truc tiep trong cua so xem ZIP.
2. Mo MO_HMOOB_VOICELAB.bat o thu muc ngoai cung.
3. Lan dau can Internet de tool tu tai va kiem tra mo hinh (~1,4 GB).
   Giu cua so lenh mo va cho trinh duyet tu mo. Khong can cai Python,
   FFmpeg, thu vien AI hay nhap API key. Cac lan sau dung ngoai tuyen.

TAO BAN DOC

- Chon Giong nu hoac Giong nam. Hai giong da co loi va doan mau dung.
- Dan loi HMONG hoac nhap tep TXT UTF-8, toi da 1.000.000 ky tu.
- Chon Nhanh (16 buoc), Chat luong (32 buoc) hoac Ban nhap (8 buoc).
- Bam Tao giong. Theo doi tien do; nghe va tai cac chuong WAV/ZIP.
- Bam Dung de giu tien do. Trong Lich su, bam Tiep tuc de doc phan con lai.

GIU DU LIEU

Giu cua so lenh trong luc dung tool. Dong trinh duyet khong dung may chu;
nhan Ctrl+C trong cua so lenh de thoat. Du lieu nam o hmoob-voicelab/.data.
Sao luu thu muc .data truoc khi xoa/chuyen/cap nhat ban Portable.
Can it nhat khoang 8 GB o dia trong, khuyen nghi RAM 16 GB va Windows x64.
CPU van dung duoc nhung toc do phu thuoc may va do dai van ban.

NEU GAP LOI

- Chay lai MO_HMOOB_VOICELAB.bat; tool dung lai cac tep da tai dung.
- Kiem tra Internet o lan chuan bi dau, dung luong dia va quyen ghi.
- Neu Windows Security chan tep, giu nguyen bao ve; khong them ngoai le
  hoac khoi phuc tep bi chan. Giu lai ten phat hien va thong tin ban tai.
- Ban ma nguon (khong co runtime/ffmpeg) dung start.bat va can Python
  3.10-3.12 64-bit, FFmpeg/ffprobe; start se tu chuan bi thu vien/mo hinh.

Nghe thu mot doan ngan truoc khi tao tai lieu dai. Mo hinh Hmong la ban
thu nghiem; khong cam ket moi tu duoc doc dung hoac giong mau tuyet doi.
