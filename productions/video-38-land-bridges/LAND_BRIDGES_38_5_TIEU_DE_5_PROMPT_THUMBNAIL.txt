1. TIÊU ĐỀ
You Could Once Walk Here. Now It's Under the Sea.
Nghĩa: Bạn từng có thể đi bộ qua đây. Giờ nơi này nằm dưới biển.

CHỮ THUMBNAIL GỢI Ý: THIS WAS LAND

PROMPT THUMBNAIL 1:
Create a striking 16:9 YouTube science-documentary thumbnail, 1280x720, photorealistic cinematic composite. From an elevated oblique aerial viewpoint, show one wide, gently sloping coastal landscape with a winding river valley. Use a clean diagonal editorial comparison: the left portion reveals the same valley as exposed grassland during an ice-age low sea level; the right portion shows it covered by deep blue seawater today, with the corresponding river channel subtly visible beneath clear shallows. Preserve a consistent terrain silhouette and camera viewpoint across both halves. This is an illustrative before-and-after comparison across different times, never a literal wall of water or instantaneous flood. Make the exposed valley the single dominant visual clue. Strong warm ochre versus cold teal contrast, realistic geology, restrained atmospheric haze, simple shapes readable on a phone. Keep the upper-left quarter visually quiet for text to be added later. No people, children, infants, adolescents, child-like figures, buildings, ancient cities, constructed bridges, migration arrows, invented archaeological finds, readable text, numbers, logos, borders or watermarks.

CHỮ THUMBNAIL GỢI Ý: WHERE DID THE SEA GO?

PROMPT THUMBNAIL 2:
Create a visually clear 16:9 YouTube science-documentary thumbnail, 1280x720, cinematic photorealistic scientific illustration. Show a vast land-based ice sheet dominating the upper-right background and an exposed continental shelf filling the foreground. A broad natural river meanders across the shelf toward a distant, visibly lower ocean shoreline. The visual story is that water stored in ice on land lowers global sea level and exposes shallow coastal ground. Show the glacier resting on continental land, never floating like an iceberg; do not show the glacier physically pushing the sea away. Use a low oblique aerial viewpoint, luminous white and blue ice, dark navy ocean, golden-brown exposed earth, crisp directional light and strong depth separation. Make the ice mass and newly exposed plain the two immediately readable elements. Leave the upper-left area uncluttered for later text. No humans, minors, silhouettes, buildings, giant arrows, impossible vertical ocean edges, literal measurement graphics, readable text, numbers, logos or watermarks. Illustrative geography only, not an authenticated reconstruction of a named site.

Base the orientation and modern continental outlines on a verified public-domain geographic reference. Keep the composition tightly focused on the connection, with clear spatial depth and uncluttered dark ocean in the upper-left for later text. CHỮ THUMBNAIL GỢI Ý: WATER STILL REMAINED

PROMPT THUMBNAIL 5:
Create an intriguing 16:9 YouTube science-documentary thumbnail, 1280x720, realistic cinematic terrain-model illustration explaining a natural barrier that remained during low sea level. Use a close oblique aerial view across two broad exposed continental shelves separated by a deep, unmistakably open blue channel containing several rugged islands. The composition must show that expanded land on both sides still does not form one continuous walking route. Represent the principle behind Sunda, Wallacea and Sahul schematically; do not imply a precise proven migration route or exact surveyed ancient coastline. Emphasize the deep blue gap as the main focal point, using warm earth on the near shelf, cooler distant land, sharp island silhouettes and a restrained shaft of sunlight across the water. Leave the upper-left area quiet for later text. The image must communicate an enduring water crossing, not a complete land connection from Asia to Australia.

HẬU KỲ THUMBNAIL
- Chỉ chọn một cặp tiêu đề–thumbnail cho mỗi lần thử; giữ đúng nội dung đã kể.
- Thêm chữ sau khi tạo ảnh: 2–5 từ, cỡ lớn, tương phản rõ; ưu tiên khả năng đọc trên màn hình điện thoại.
- Kiểm tra hướng và hình dạng địa lý nếu dùng cặp 4. Không đăng bản đồ AI sai hướng hoặc sai vị trí.
- Bối cảnh trước–sau và cửa sổ nhìn xuống đáy biển là minh họa biên tập, không phải cảnh quay hiện vật hay bằng chứng khảo cổ thật.
