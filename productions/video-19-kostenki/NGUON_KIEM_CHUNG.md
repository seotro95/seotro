# Nguồn và giới hạn bằng chứng

- **S01 — Pryor et al.2020. The chronology and function of a new circular mammoth-bone structure at Kostenki11**: https://doi.org/10.15184/aqy.2020.7
  Chronology,function,flotation and activity intensity; new wording, no figures or long quotations copied.

- **S02 — University of Exeter. Mysterious bone circles,17March2020**: https://news-archive.exeter.ac.uk/2020/march/title_782319_en.html
  Institutional explanation of51mandibles,64skulls,charred plants,wood,bone and flint chips. The old press text has dimensional/date simplifications; script uses roughly12m/25ka from studies, not its30ft wording.

- **S03 — Rey-Iglesia et al.2025. Ancient biomolecular analysis of39mammoth individuals from Kostenki11-Ia**: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.qeh.2024.100049
  39individuals,30confident sex assignments:17female/13male; older mandibles,collection of already-old bones,chronological limits. CC BY-NC-ND; factual reference only, original narration, no source text/figures/PDF redistributed.

- **S04 — Chu,Shydlovskyi and Maier2025. Revised radiocarbon chronology at Mezhyrich**: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12639289/
  CC BY. Small-animal dates and structure4 context around18ka; age range18,248–17,764calBP and model span0–429years do not prove429years of continuous occupation. Original narrative interpretation, no figures copied.

- **S05 — Iakovleva. The mammoth bone dwellings of Gontsy,a first synthesis**: https://arheologia.com.ua/index.php/arheologia/article/view/45
  Settlement-scale comparison:bone structures,pits,hearths,work and disposal areas; do not transfer its reconstructed designs to Kostenki.

- **S06 — Iakovleva2015. Architecture of mammoth bone circular dwellings**: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1040618214006247
  Comparative architecture and interpretations; interpretations are not universal proven functions. Abstract consulted, no unaccessed detailed claims.

- **S07 — Oxford Radiocarbon Accelerator Unit. Methods and results**: https://www.arch.ox.ac.uk/orau-methods-and-results
  Biological event dated,calibration,contamination and sample selection; bone reflects last part of animal life,wood dates growth; no assertion of an exact date of death.

- **S08 — Penn State Radiocarbon Laboratory. Guide to radiocarbon sampling**: https://iee.psu.edu/labs/radiocarbon-laboratory/guide-radiocarbon-sampling
  Context,inbuilt age and old wood; sample ages are indirectly related to archaeological events.
