HMONG STUDIO 0.4.1 - NHAN BAN GIONG CUC BO

1. Cai Python 3.12 64-bit; giai nen vao thu muc moi.
2. Chay install_clone_ai.bat MOT LAN, cho tai thu vien va khoang 5.4 GB mo hinh.
3. Bo cai thanh cong thi chay start.bat; Ctrl+F5 neu dang mo trang cu.
4. Chon mau NAM hoac NU, nhap chu Hmong, chon cach doc, bam TAO VOICE.
5. Nut DUNG se dung tien trinh; nghe va tai WAV khi hoan tat.

Khong can micro, API key, dich vu tra phi hoac ban chep mau.
Can RAM khoang 16 GB va cho trong it nhat 15 GB. CPU co the cham.
install_local_ai.bat nay cung mo bo cai nhan ban. MMS rieng: install_mms_asr.bat.
Da tao WAV that bang ca hai mau tren Linux CPU; chua thu Windows that.
Nghe kiem tra phat am va do giong giong truoc khi su dung.
Xem README.md de biet chi tiet va ket qua kiem thu.

Bo cai tao moi truong rieng, khong ghi de .venv cu.
