- Giam bang chu Qwen giu tren GPU khoang 0.507 GiB; cac phan tao giong van CUDA.
- Chua co so do Windows/GPU thuc, CHUA DAT 2 phut tao 1 gio voice.
- Loi do/cancel se giu cau hinh truoc. Mau ngan khong phai tao du 1 gio.
